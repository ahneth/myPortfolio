Global Market Ticker Formats (Yahoo Finance):
  • Hong Kong (HKEX): Use .HK suffix — e.g. 2800.HK (Tracker Fund), 0700.HK (Tencent), 0005.HK (HSBC). Auto-normalizes .HKG or 700.HK automatically.
  • United States (NYSE / NASDAQ): Standard symbol without suffix — e.g. AAPL, NVDA, MSFT, VOO, BRK-B.
  • London (LSE): Use .L suffix — e.g. HSBA.L (HSBC UK), SHEL.L (Shell), AZN.L (AstraZeneca), VUAG.L (Vanguard S&P 500). Auto-normalizes .LON to .L.
